how to play

How to Play

SurvivorsRepair generators, heal teammates, drop pallets, vault windows, open the gate, and leave.
KillerTrack survivors, hold M1 for a lunge, kick generators, break pallets, hook or execute downed survivors.
ControlsWASD move, mouse aim, Space vault/drop/break, E interact, Shift sprint, R chat wheel.
WinSurvivors win by escaping. Killer wins by removing everyone from the board, because apparently circles can have consequences.
